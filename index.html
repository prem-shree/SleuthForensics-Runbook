Sleuth Forensics
Internal Runbook & Engagement Platform

An internal platform that answers one question for a consultant at any point in any engagement: what exactly do I need to do next to execute this correctly?

No application code has been written. Everything here is a specification, and nothing in it is SME-validated. Seven governing constraints issued by Sleuth are binding on all of it — see 17 · Governing constraints.
